Miracle Spritz pH 4.6 multi-surface cleaner

Miracle Spritz is our own formula, a pH 4.6 multi-surface cleaner made and packaged in Clovis, California. It is an alternative to petroleum-solvent and caustic cleaners for vehicles, maintenance garages, shops, offices and solar panels. U.S. registered trademark No. 6,574,238.

  • Sizes: 16 oz and 32 oz spray bottles, 1-gallon bottles, 5-gallon carboys and 55-gallon drums
  • Used on show cars, fleet vehicles, facility surfaces and solar panels
  • Spray on, wipe off
  • 24 to 48 hour delivery across the Central Valley from our Clovis plant

Miracle Spritz is a surface cleaner. It is not a disinfectant or a pesticide, and we make no germ-killing claims.
